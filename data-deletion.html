Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Heartland Engineering operates a self-hosted social media scheduling service that connects to social platform accounts (including Meta platforms) at the account owner's request. We do not operate a public sign-up service, and we do not sell or share personal information.

How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of any data associated with your account, email chuck@heartland-eng.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the social account handle or email address associated with the data.

What we delete

Upon a verified request we delete:

  • Stored OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for the connected account
  • Basic profile information (account name, handle, profile identifier) received from the connected platform
  • Any scheduled or draft post content created through the service for that account
  • Any records associating the account with our scheduling service

Timeline

Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Where content has already been published to a third-party platform, removing it from that platform is subject to that platform's own controls; we will remove our stored copies and revoke our access.

Revoking access

You may also revoke this application's access at any time from your social platform's settings (for example, Meta's "Apps and Websites" section). Revoking access immediately ends our ability to publish on your behalf.

Contact

Questions about data deletion: chuck@heartland-eng.com
